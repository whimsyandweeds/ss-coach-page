Logistics Exit Concierge

She is ready to leave.
The logistics are not.

I handle the part that stalls the exit — so you can stay in yours.

I'm Tonya. I'm a survivor, and I built Sophie + Sage for the women your clients are: the ones who have decided to go, and then hit the wall of housing applications, utility calls, movers, deposits, and paperwork. That wall is my whole career.

Two ways to read this — pick the one that fits you.

Why I built this

When I decided to go, I went

The decision was never the part that needed handling. The logistics were.

I'm not a coach, and I won't try to talk anyone into leaving — that work belongs to the coaches who do it best. My work starts after the decision: the apartment hunt, the deposits, the utility transfers, the movers, the forty phone calls on hold. That's where exits stall, and it's exactly the work I've done professionally for twenty years.

Sophie + Sage is a logistics exit concierge. A coach refers a client, and I take the entire administrative weight of the move off both of you — quietly, behind the scenes, on a secure channel. She focuses on her safety and her next chapter. You keep the relationship and the coaching. I do the hold music.

Built for the monitored

When she can't call herself

Some of the women this is for are being watched. They can't safely spend an afternoon calling landlords, and they can't be the voice on the phone with a utility company. So the setup runs through you: you come to me through a secure channel, and I make the calls she can't make. No direct contact with her until safe-channel consent. Zero footprint by default — that's not a feature of this service, it's the point of it.
