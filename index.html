The logistics are not.

I handle the part that stalls the exit — so you can stay in yours.

For coaches whose clients have decided to go. I take the housing, utilities, movers, deposits, and every call she can’t safely make.

Why this exists

I already knew how to leave.

I’d spent years on the property and accounting side. I knew who to call, what to turn on, and how to move. Most women don’t have that in their pocket. That’s why exits stall after the decision is made. I want to be that person for them.

What I take

The whole logistics load.

Housing search, applications, deposits — to keys in hand.
Utilities on, utilities off, DV waivers flagged and filed.
Movers and storage sequenced around her safety plan.
A real ninety-day money map for rent, deposits, and fees.
Every call she cannot safely make herself.
